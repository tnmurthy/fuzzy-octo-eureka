## 🧪 6. Candidate Qualification Rubric (20-Minute Technical Screen)

The landing page guarantees a **20-minute technical screen** with a full refund if not a fit. This maintains high cohort quality and peer prestige:

### Screening Rubric:
1. **Infrastructure Fundamentals (5 mins):**
   - *"Walk us through how traffic moves from an ALB to an ECS/K8s service in your current production stack."*
   - *"How do you currently handle secret injection in CI/CD?"*
2. **Security Intuition (10 mins):**
   - *"If a container begins making outbound SSH connections to an unknown public IP at 2 AM, what is your first response step?"*
   - *"Explain the difference between authentication and authorization in AWS IAM role assumption."*
3. **Commitment & Fit (5 mins):**
   - *"The program runs 3.5 hours per week over 6 months with hands-on lab milestones. Does your current work schedule permit 3 hours of lab work on weekends?"*

**Scoring Gate:** Score $\ge 7/10$ receives an immediate admission confirmation and lab credential provisioning.
